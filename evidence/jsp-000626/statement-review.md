[Original question](https://www.erdosproblems.com/764).

The selected target is the negative answer for ordered three-term representation sums: no A⊆ℕ and c>0 have cumulative triple convolution through N equal to cN+O(1). The source cites Vaughan and proves the bounded-error special case needed here. This packet does not claim the full stronger quantitative error theorem from the cited paper.
