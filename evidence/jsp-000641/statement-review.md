# Erdős 781: selected statement review

[Original question](https://www.erdosproblems.com/781).

A descending wave is a strictly increasing sequence of positions with nonincreasing successive gaps. The source treats two colors, constructs the exact least forcing interval length, and supplies upper/lower cubic bounds for sufficiently large k. It also refutes the proposed universal quadratic identity k²−k+1. Indexing a finite interval from zero instead of one does not change colorings or successive gaps. The result is Θ(k³) with explicit coarse constants and threshold; it is not an exact formula at every k.
